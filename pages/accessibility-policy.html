Website Accessibility Matters

Warf Designs LLC is committed to providing an accessible website experience and aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. We regularly review the site for keyboard access, readable contrast, clear structure, accessible forms, and compatibility with assistive technology.

If you experience trouble navigating, viewing, or otherwise accessing content on this website, call or text us at 740-438-7646. Please tell us which page or feature caused difficulty and which assistive technology or browser you used, if applicable. If you require content in an alternate format, let us know and we will make a reasonable effort to provide it.

We welcome any and all suggestions for improving our website’s accessibility. Please do not hesitate to reach out with any questions, comments, or concerns.
